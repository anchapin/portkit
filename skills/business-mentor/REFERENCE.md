# Business Mentor Reference

## Frameworks

### Lean Canvas (Ash Maurya)
1. **Problem** — Top 3 pain points
2. **Customer** — Segmented target market
3. **Unique Value Proposition** — Clear, compelling statement
4. **Solution** — Top 3 features
5. **Unfair Advantage** — Cannot be easily copied
6. **Channels** — Path to customers
7. **Metrics** — Key vanity/actionable metrics
8. **Cost Structure** — Fixed/variable breakdown
9. **Revenue Streams** — Pricing, LTV, CAC

### Business Model Canvas (Osterwalder)
Similar but focuses on infrastructure, partnerships, and relationships.

### SWOT Analysis
| | Helpful | Harmful |
|---|---|---|
| **Internal** | Strengths | Weaknesses |
| **External** | Opportunities | Threats |

### Unit Economics
- **CAC** — Customer Acquisition Cost
- **LTV** — Lifetime Value (target LTV:CAC ≥ 3:1)
- **Payback Period** — Months to recover CAC (target ≤ 12 months)
- **MRR/ARR** — Monthly/Annual Recurring Revenue

### Startup Metrics by Stage

| Stage | Key Metric |
|---|---|
| Idea | Problem interview responses |
| MVP | Activation rate, time-to-value |
| Early | Retention curve, NPS |
| Growth | LTV:CAC, viral coefficient |
| Scale | Net revenue retention, payback period |

## Pricing Frameworks

### 1. Cost-Plus
`Price = Cost × (1 + Margin%)`
Simple but ignores value and competition.

### 2. Competitive
Price based on market rates. Use when:
- Commoditized market
- Price-sensitive customers
- Entering established market

### 3. Value-Based
`Price = Value Delivered × Capture Rate`
Best when you can quantify customer ROI. Target 10-30% value capture.

### 4. Freemium
- Free tier drives adoption
- Paid tier captures willingness to pay
- Viral loop for distribution

## Go-To-Market Frameworks

### 7Ps of Marketing
Product, Price, Place, Promotion, People, Process, Physical Evidence

### Bullseye Framework (Andrew Chen)
1. **Inner ring** — Primary channel (proven, scalable)
2. **Middle ring** — Back-up channels (working but not scalable)
3. **Outer ring** — Experimental channels (early signals)

Focus on ONE primary channel until dominant.

### Poached & Disturbed
- Poached: competitor's customers with better offering
- Disturbed: adjacent market entering your space

## Growth Frameworks

### AARRR (Pirate Metrics)
Acquisition → Activation → Retention → Referral → Revenue

### Viral Loop Anatomy
1. Core value proposition
2. Sharing mechanism
3. Incentive structure
4. Friction removal

### Retention Curves
- **Sticky**: log curve (Slack, Notion) — focus on engagement
- **Churning**: step function (E-commerce) — focus on re-engagement
- **Power law**: top 20% customers drive 80% revenue — focus on NPS

## Case Studies

### Dropbox (Viral Loops)
- Referral program: free storage for both referrer and referee
- Result: 3900% growth in 15 months

### Airbnb (Trust & Supply)
- Professional photography for listings
- Craigslist cross-posting
- Key insight: quality supply unlocks demand

### Slack (Product-Led Growth)
- Free tier with conversion
- Bottom-up adoption (teams, not enterprises)
- Result: fastest SaaS to $1B ARR

### Notion (Developer Evangelism)
- Community-first growth
- Template library
- Key insight: users become advocates
