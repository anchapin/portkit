## 3. Description of the Service

PortKit accepts a Minecraft Java Edition mod (commonly distributed as a `.jar` or `.zip` archive) and uses a multi-agent AI pipeline to attempt to produce a Bedrock Edition add-on (commonly a `.mcaddon` or `.zip` archive) that approximates the original mod's behavior, assets, and features.

PortKit is provided as a productivity and prototyping aid. The output is generated by an AI model and, where the input mod is not open source, may not be a faithful, complete, or functional conversion. You are responsible for reviewing any output before you use, distribute, or rely on it.
