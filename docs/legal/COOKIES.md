# Cookie Policy

## 1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how PortKit uses cookies and similar tracking technologies when you use our mod conversion service. We are committed to being transparent about how we use these technologies and respecting your privacy choices.

## 2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences, understand how you use the site, and improve your overall experience.

## 3. Cookies We Use

### 3.1 Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly and cannot be disabled without affecting the service.

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration |
|-------------|---------|----------|
| `session_id` | Maintains your authenticated session across pages | Session |
| `csrf_token` | Protects against cross-site request forgery attacks | Session |
| `conversion_job` | Tracks your active conversion job state | 24 hours |

**Legal Basis**: These cookies are essential for providing the service you requested. Under GDPR, they fall under legitimate interest or contract necessity (Art. 6(1)(b)).

### 3.2 Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting anonymous information about pages visited, time spent, and errors encountered.

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Provider |
|-------------|---------|----------|
| `_ga` | Distinguishes unique users via Google Analytics | Google |
| `_gid` | Distinguishes unique users within a 24-hour window | Google |
| `_gat` | Rate-limits request frequency to Google Analytics | Google |

**Legal Basis**: Under GDPR and CCPA, analytics cookies require explicit consent (opt-in). You can manage these via our cookie consent banner or opt-out mechanisms listed in Section 5.

### 3.3 Functional Cookies

These cookies enable enhanced features and personalization to improve your experience.

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration |
|-------------|---------|----------|
| `preferences` | Stores your UI preferences (e.g., theme, language) | 1 year |
| `conversion_history` | Remembers your recent conversions for quick access | 30 days |

**Legal Basis**: These cookies require consent under GDPR Art. 6(1)(a).

### 3.4 Authentication Cookies

When you create an account or log in, we may use authentication cookies to verify your identity.

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration |
|-------------|---------|----------|
| `auth_token` | Securely authenticates your session | 7 days |
| `refresh_token` | Extends your session automatically | 30 days |

**Legal Basis**: Required for account functionality under contract necessity (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).

## 5. Your Choices / Opt-Out

You have several options to control cookies:

### 5.1 Cookie Consent Banner
When you first visit our site, you will see a cookie consent banner where you can:
- Accept all cookies
- Reject non-essential cookies
- Customize your preferences by category

### 5.2 Browser Settings
Most web browsers allow you to:
- View what cookies are stored on your device
- Delete all or specific cookies
- Block cookies from all or certain websites
- Block third-party cookies
- Clear all cookies when you close your browser

Instructions for managing cookies in common browsers:
- [Google Chrome](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/61416)
- [Mozilla Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/cookies-information-websites-store-on-your-computer)
- [Safari](https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/manage-cookies-sfri11471/mac)
- [Microsoft Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/delete-cookies-in-microsoft-edge-63947406-40ac-c3b8-57b9-2a946a29ae09)

### 5.3 Google Analytics Opt-Out
To opt out of Google Analytics tracking, you can:
1. Use our cookie consent banner to disable analytics cookies
2. Install the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout)

### 5.4 Global Privacy Control
California residents may activate the "Global Privacy Control" (GPC) signal in supported browsers to automatically opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information.

## 6. Do Not Track

We respect the "Do Not Track" browser setting. If you have DNT enabled in your browser, we will not set analytics or marketing cookies.
